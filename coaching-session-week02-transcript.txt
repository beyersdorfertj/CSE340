Exactly right, Thomas. <% runs JavaScript logic silently (the loop, the

an expression and prints its escaped value into the HTML.

And to complete the trio: that <%- on line 1 is the third variant—it

it harmless as text instead of executing it—basic XSS protection you're
getting for free. You chose the right tag in every spot. Topic 4: covered.
